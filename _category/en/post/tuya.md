description: "Tuya is a Chinese IoT platform that powers thousands of connected devices: plugs, bulbs, sensors, pool equipment and household appliances. Many brands (Smart Life, LSC, Nedis, Poolex, Nous...) rely on the same Tuya cloud and the same chips, which is why a single app — Smart Life or Tuya Smart — can control devices from many different manufacturers.
<br />
For Home Assistant users, Tuya devices can be integrated in two ways: through the official Tuya integration, which relies on the cloud and therefore on an internet connection, or through community solutions such as LocalTuya and Tuya Local (HACS), which talk directly to the device on the local network. The local approach requires the device_id and the local_key of each device, but it removes any dependency on the manufacturer's servers and dramatically reduces latency.
<br />
This category gathers tutorials and feedback on Tuya devices and on their local integration into Home Assistant."
